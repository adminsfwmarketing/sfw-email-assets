Celebrating David’s Retirement

Written by Audrey on October 30, David’s last day.

Dear Clients and Friends,

Today marks a very special milestone as David Fox-Revett officially retires after 32 and a half years with IG.

Over the course of his career, David has had the privilege of helping generations of families build, protect, and enjoy their wealth. Through changing markets, life’s major milestones, and countless conversations around kitchen tables and office desks, David has remained unwavering in his commitment to serving his clients with integrity, compassion, and genuine care.

More than anything, David has always valued the relationships he built with each of you. The trust you placed in him over the years was never taken for granted, and it has been one of the greatest honours of his career to walk alongside you and your families through the many chapters of life.

Last night, we had the opportunity to celebrate David’s remarkable career surrounded by family, friends, colleagues, and members of the IG community. The evening was filled with stories, laughter, gratitude, and reflection on the profound impact he has had on so many lives.

A few photos from the celebration and from throughout David’s journey:

David and Audrey at an IG event, representing the continuation of a legacy built on trusted relationships and client-first advice.

One of David’s favourite places to recharge and reflect, enjoying the outdoors he loves so much.

David addressing family, friends, clients, and colleagues during his retirement celebration.

Shaun, David and Audrey at a conference in St. John’s, Newfoundland.

As David turns the page to a new chapter, he does so knowing the practice and the clients he cares so deeply about are in good hands. Audrey and Shaun are proud to continue serving your families with the same dedication, professionalism, and personal attention that David brought to his work every day.

On behalf of all of us, thank you for your trust, loyalty, and friendship over the years. Your support helped make David’s career so meaningful, and for that, our family is profoundly grateful.

Please join us in wishing David and Margaret many years of happiness, good health, adventure, fishing trips, and time spent with the people he loves most.

David, congratulations on an extraordinary career and a retirement that is so richly deserved.

With sincere gratitude,

Audrey & Shaun

P.S. Well done, Dad. Enjoy every minute of this next chapter. You’ve earned it.

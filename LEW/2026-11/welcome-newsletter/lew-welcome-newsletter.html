YOUR YEAR-END PLANNING CHECKLIST

Several important tax and financial planning opportunities must be acted on by December 31. Here are the key items to review before year-end:

  • Charitable donations: gifts must be made by December 31 to be claimed on this year’s tax return. Donating eligible investments instead of cash can bring added tax savings.
  • Tax-loss selling: selling non-registered investments held elsewhere that have lost value may help offset capital gains, as long as trades settle by December 31. Our team is happy to help you decide whether this strategy makes sense for you.
  • RESP contributions: contribute by December 31 to receive this year’s Canada Education Savings Grant.
  • FHSA contributions: unlike RRSPs, contributions to a First Home Savings Account must be made by December 31 to count for this tax year.
  • TFSA withdrawals: planning to withdraw early next year? Withdrawing before December 31 means that room comes back on January 1, rather than a year later.
  • Beneficiaries and wills: if your family situation changed this year, make sure your beneficiary designations and will still reflect your wishes.
  • Looking ahead: the RRSP contribution deadline for the 2026 tax year is March 1, 2027. Starting early can make it easier.

Not sure which of these apply to you? Reply to this email or call our office and we’ll go through it together.

Meet our speakers

Dr. Zahinoor Ismail, MD, FRCPC

Dr. Ismail is a neuropsychiatrist and clinician scientist at the University of Calgary, where he is a professor in the Cumming School of Medicine and a member of the Hotchkiss Brain Institute. With more than 20 years of clinical experience in neuropsychiatry and geriatric psychiatry, his research looks for early signs of dementia, including changes in mood and behaviour that can appear before memory loss. He chairs the Canadian Conference on Dementia and is a scientific adviser to Gordie Howe CARES.

Brad Lohman

Brad brings more than 30 years of nursing experience to the conversation. He is co-founder of Vytality at Home and Board Chair of Gordie Howe CARES, where he has spent more than a decade building programs for people living with dementia and the family members who care for them, including the Caregiver and Patient Support (CAPS) Program.

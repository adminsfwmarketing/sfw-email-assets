Scams have become harder to spot. A text that looks like it’s from your bank, a call from someone who says they’re with the CRA, an investment opportunity from a new online friend: they’re more convincing than ever, and they reach people who are careful and well-informed.

The good news is that while the tactics keep changing, the warning signs stay remarkably consistent. Once you know what to look for, it becomes much easier to pause, check and protect yourself.

That’s why we’d like to invite you to Protecting Your Financial Future: A practical guide to avoiding scams on Thursday, November 19. Shane Curtis, who leads IG Wealth Management’s Fraud Risk Management Program, and Herp Lamba, Senior Vice-President, Financial Services, will walk through the scams Canadians see most often, the red flags they share, and the practical steps to take if something feels off.

It’s a useful hour for anyone, and a good one to share with parents, adult children or friends who might be targeted.

Protecting Your Financial Future:

A practical guide to avoiding scams
 

Scams constantly change. Their red flags don’t.

Join Herp Lamba, Senior Vice-President, Financial Services, Central and Western Canada, and Shane Curtis, Assistant Vice-President of Risk Management at IG Wealth Management, for a plain-language session on avoiding modern scams. You’ll learn:

- The four types of scams Canadians face most often, with real-world examples

- The red flags that show up across every scam type and how to quickly recognize them

- Practical steps to protect yourself: what to do when something feels off, how to verify independently and who to contact if you’ve been impacted

- How your IG Advisor can support you, because you don’t need to navigate this alone

The webinar will conclude with a virtual Q&A session.

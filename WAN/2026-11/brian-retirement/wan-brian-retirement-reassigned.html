A personal announcement from Brian
With gratitude for 28½ wonderful years

Dear Valued Clients and Friends,

After 28½ wonderful and rewarding years with IG Wealth Management, I want to personally share some important news with you. I have decided to retire on January 31, 2027, bringing to a close a career that has been one of the most meaningful chapters of my life.

Over these years, I’ve had the privilege of working with extraordinary individuals and families, many of whom have been with me for decades. Together, we’ve navigated changing markets, celebrated personal and financial milestones, planned for retirements and worked toward lasting security for future generations. One of the greatest privileges of my career has been working with multiple generations of the same families, from helping young couples take their first financial steps to watching children and grandchildren build futures of their own.

I have never taken lightly the responsibility of being trusted with your financial well-being. Behind every financial plan is a family, a dream or a lifetime of hard work, and being invited into those decisions has been an honour. What I will treasure most are the relationships, friendships and trust we’ve built along the way. For that, I am truly grateful.

100 years of IG, and the right time for a new chapter.

Debbie and I celebrated IG Wealth Management’s 100th anniversary celebration in Winnipeg, with IG colleagues and friends from across the country. It was an inspiring reminder of the strength, stability and commitment to clients that have defined this organization for a century.

I leave this profession with real confidence in IG’s future, from its financial planning expertise and resources to the talented people who support our clients every day. Having just marked 100 years of IG’s history, I can’t think of a more fitting milestone to begin my own next chapter.

A thoughtful, seamless transition

As I prepare for retirement, one of my greatest priorities is making sure that each of you continues to receive the personal attention, professional advice and dedicated service you’ve come to expect.

With that in mind, I’ve thought carefully about where each family lives and the kind of planning support that will serve them best in the years ahead. For you, that means working with an experienced and highly qualified IG Advisor in your own community. This is someone close enough to sit down with you in person, and whose expertise fits your planning needs.

These arrangements weren’t made lightly. Every client relationship has been equally meaningful to me, and I’ve approached each one with the same care I’d want for my own family. I want you to feel confident that your financial future remains in excellent hands.

On a personal note, perhaps the clearest sign of my confidence in IG is that my own family’s investments will continue to be managed through IG throughout our retirement. I can think of no greater endorsement.

We’ll be reaching out to you personally to introduce your new consultant, answer your questions and help make this transition as comfortable and seamless as possible.

A heartfelt thank you

Thank you for letting me be part of your lives, for sharing your dreams and for placing your trust in me through so many stages of your financial journey. It has been an honour to serve you and our community.

After so many years helping others prepare for retirement, it’s time for me to start enjoying my own, with more time for family and travel with Debbie. I do so with a grateful heart, knowing you’ll continue to be supported by an exceptional organization and dedicated professionals.

With sincere appreciation and warmest wishes,

Brian

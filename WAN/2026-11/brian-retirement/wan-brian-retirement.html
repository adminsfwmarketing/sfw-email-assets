A note from Brian on his retirement:

For 28 years, I’ve had the privilege of doing work I love in a community I’m proud to call home. Today I’m writing to share some personal news. After a lot of thought, I’ve decided it’s time to retire from IG Private Wealth Management, effective January 21, 2027.

It wasn’t an easy decision, but I’m making it with real confidence because I know who will be looking after you.

Over the years I’ve had the privilege of working with so many clients, families and business owners here in Fort McMurray. This is a community where relationships matter and people show up for one another. Many of you have been with me through new jobs, growing businesses, kids leaving home and retirements of your own. Being part of those moments has been the best part of this work.

Arun, Stephanie and our team bring so much care, experience and energy to the practice, and they already know many of you well. I couldn’t be leaving it in better hands. The team you know will still be here, and you can reach them the same way you always have.

The thing I most want you to know is that I’m staying on as a client of Arun’s. When it came time to decide who I’d trust with my own financial plan, the choice was easy. I hope that gives you the same confidence it gives me.

If you have any questions, please reach out. I’d be glad to hear from you.

Thank YOU to our clients, colleagues, friends and community for 28 wonderful years. It’s been an honour.

Sincerely,

Brian,

on behalf of Arun, Stephanie,
and the team behind the scenes at Wanner, Nair & Associates

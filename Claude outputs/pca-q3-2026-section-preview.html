Build, partner, buy, finance or sell? Your next stage of growth

Tariffs have pushed many Canadian business owners to focus on protecting margins, customers and cash flow. That matters, but defence shouldn’t be the whole strategy. The Q3 2026 newsletter from IG Private Company Advisory looks at where a business’s next stage of growth could come from, and who is best positioned to pursue it.

The article walks through the trade-offs of building, partnering or acquiring, how growth can sometimes be financed with debt rather than by selling equity, and why a sale can be worth evaluating even when a company is performing well.

The takeaway: before committing to the next stage, find out what your business can prudently borrow and what a credible buyer might pay. Those two numbers can change the decision. If you own a business and would like to talk through your options, we’re happy to help.
